Lasagna

Description

Lasagna layers pasta, rich meat sauce, creamy ricotta, and melted cheese into a comforting baked dish. It is a classic family meal that rewards slow simmering and generous assembly.

This version focuses on balanced flavor and a sturdy texture so each slice holds together cleanly on the plate.

Ingredients

  • Lasagna noodles
  • Ground beef
  • Marinara sauce
  • Ricotta cheese
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Parmesan cheese
  • Egg
  • Garlic
  • Salt and pepper

Steps

  1. Cook the noodles according to the package directions.
  2. Brown the beef with garlic, salt, and pepper, then stir in the marinara sauce.
  3. Mix ricotta with the egg and a little Parmesan.
  4. Layer sauce, noodles, ricotta, and mozzarella in a baking dish.
  5. Repeat the layers, finish with cheese, and bake until bubbling and golden.
  6. Let the lasagna rest before slicing and serving.
